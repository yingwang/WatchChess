# Privacy Policy

**WatchChess** (`com.yingwang.watchchess`)
Last updated: 20 September 2026

## The short version

WatchChess collects nothing, stores nothing about you, and sends nothing
anywhere. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no network
code of any kind.

## What the app can do on your device

The only permission WatchChess requests is `VIBRATE`, used for the small haptic
tick when the crown steps the highlight from one piece to the next and when a
move is played. It requests no internet permission at all, so it is not
technically capable of transmitting anything even if it wanted to.

The chess engine runs entirely on the watch as a local process. Your games are
never uploaded, analysed, or shared. Nothing is written to disk except the
engine's own working files, which contain no personal information.

## Data collected

None.

- No personal or identifying information
- No location, contacts, calendar, health or sensor data
- No usage statistics, crash reporting or analytics
- No advertising identifiers
- No cookies or trackers

## Third parties

There are none. No SDKs, no services, no data processors. The bundled engine is
[Fairy-Stockfish](https://github.com/fairy-stockfish/Fairy-Stockfish), which runs
locally and has no network access either.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including
children.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be committed to this repository, so its history is
public and auditable.
